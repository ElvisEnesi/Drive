CUSTECH CAMPUS TRANSIT — MULTI-PAGE FRONTEND

Files: separate HTML page for every sidebar destination, shared style.css, shared script.js.

Run: extract the ZIP, open this folder in VS Code, then open index.html in your browser or use Live Server.

Pages included: Overview, Track rides, Order a ride, Ride requests, All rides, Past rides, Drivers, Students & clients, Admins & staff, Routes & stops, Fleet & vehicles, Schedules, Payments, Reports & analytics, Support tickets, Settings.

IMPORTANT: This is a frontend prototype with sample data. Form submissions and request actions are demo-only and are not stored. Live GPS, authentication, permissions, and persistent records require a PHP backend and database integration.
